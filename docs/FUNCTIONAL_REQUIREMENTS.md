# Functional requirements

## FEAT-CATALOG

### FR-CATALOG-1

A visitor can browse the product catalog and filter it by category.

### FR-CATALOG-2

A visitor can open a product's detail page and see its price, description,
and availability.

## FEAT-CART

### FR-CART-1

A visitor can add a product to the cart and see the cart's item count update.

### FR-CART-2

A visitor can change a cart item's quantity or remove it, and the cart total
updates to match.

## FEAT-CHECKOUT

### FR-CHECKOUT-1

A visitor can complete checkout by entering shipping and payment details, and
receives an order confirmation showing exactly what they ordered.
