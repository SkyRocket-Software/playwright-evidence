# Sample Shop

Sample Shop lets a visitor browse a product catalog, build a cart, and check
out without creating an account.

## FEAT-CATALOG: Browsing the product catalog

A visitor can browse the product catalog, filter it by category, and open a
product's detail page to see its price, description, and availability.

## FEAT-CART: Managing the shopping cart

A visitor can add a product to the cart, change an item's quantity, and remove
an item, with the cart total updating to match.

## FEAT-CHECKOUT: Completing checkout

A visitor can enter shipping and payment details, place an order, and receive
a confirmation showing exactly what they ordered.
